# CBRE Australia

> CBRE Australia is the Australian arm of CBRE Group, the global commercial real estate services and investment firm, trading locally as CBRE (Gwsla) Pty Ltd (AU09949) and operating across the Pacific from cbre.com.au. It works on the advisory and management side of the Australian property value chain: capital markets and investment sales, leasing and occupier advisory, valuation and advisory, property and asset management, and project and design-and-build delivery across office, retail, industrial and logistics, build-to-rent, and alternative assets.

> IMPORTANT FOR AGENTS: CBRE Australia publishes **no public API**. As of 26 July 2026 there is no developer portal, no API documentation, no machine-readable contract (OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL, OData `$metadata`, WSDL of CBRE's own authorship), no SDK, no Postman collection, no webhooks, and no self-serve onboarding. `developer.cbre.com`, `developers.cbre.com`, `developer.cbre.com.au`, `api.cbre.com.au`, and `docs.cbre.com.au` are all NXDOMAIN. `api.cbre.com` resolves but sits behind an Imperva WAF that returns 403 to every anonymous request. `api-dev.cbre.com` serves a default WSO2 API Manager landing page with no CBRE APIs deployed. `eipportal.cbre.com` is a login-gated "Integration Platform" SPA. Access to CBRE data in Australia is a commercial client or partner engagement negotiated offline, not a developer signup. Do not attempt to synthesize or assume an API surface for this provider. CBRE's Terms of Use explicitly prohibit spidering, screen scraping, and database scraping of property listings, and `robots.txt` disallows `/api`.

## Sector context

- RESO is absent from CBRE Australia entirely, and this is the correct answer for the market: Australia has no MLS, no RESO-certified Australian organization appears in the public RESO certification listing, and RESO Web API / Data Dictionary certification is an artifact of the North American MLS system.
- Australia's mandated, national, machine-readable property rail is PEXA's electronic conveyancing network — a settlement rail, not a listing standard, and not something CBRE Australia publishes an interface to.
- Australian commercial listing distribution flows through realcommercial.com.au (REA Group) and agency websites; residential runs through the realestate.com.au / Domain duopoly.
